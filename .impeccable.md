# Vela — Design context (Impeccable)

## Design Context

### Users

**Who:** Young adults (18–30) shopping for sportswear.

**Context:** Primarily **mobile**, in short windows—commute, lunch break, post-workout. They scan quickly, decide fast, and leave if the experience feels cluttered or slow. They expect the store to feel as intentional as the brand.

**Jobs to be done:**

1. Find the right product (style, size, color) with minimal friction.
2. Complete the purchase as fast as possible once decided.

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** Minimal. Clean. Confident.

**Emotional goal:** **Calm confidence.** The store knows what it is—no urgency tricks, no noise. The **product is the hero**; the UI stays out of the way.

**Product positioning:** Vela is an **open-source eCommerce storefront template** on the Medusa stack, packaged as a **Railway one-click deploy for developers**. The demo personality is a **fashion sportswear** brand (“Vela”) so the template ships as a credible real store while staying flexible for other commerce use cases.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Theme:** **Light only** (no dark mode as the default product experience).

**References (what to steal):**

- **Shopify Release** — Editorial feel, full-width imagery, clean type hierarchy, confident spacing; visuals lead before grids.
- **Shopify Ignite** — Energetic but not chaotic; strong balance of lifestyle photography and listing UX; thoughtful collection filtering and product cards.

**Extract specifically:** Generous white space, large product imagery, typography carrying structure, very few competing elements per screen.

**Anti-references:** Anything like Amazon, ASOS sale pages, or AliExpress—dense grids, badge overload (“HOT”, “SALE”, “LAST ONE”), competing CTAs, information density that overwhelms.

**Color & surfaces:** Build on **Medusa UI semantic colors** via `@medusajs/ui-preset`—use tokenized utilities (e.g. `bg-ui-bg-base`, `text-ui-fg-base`, `text-ui-fg-subtle`, `border-ui-border-base`, `bg-ui-bg-subtle`, `bg-ui-bg-field`) rather than ad-hoc hex values. Layer **editorial, photography-led** presentation on that neutral base: let campaign and product imagery provide warmth and energy; keep UI chrome quiet and consistent.

### Accessibility & Quality Bar

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** as baseline.
- **Contrast:** ≥ 4.5:1 for body text; ≥ 3:1 for large text and UI components.
- **Never color-only state:** Errors, out-of-stock, and active selection need text, icons, or patterns—not hue alone.
- **Keyboard:** Full keyboard support for cart drawer, modals, size/color selectors, and filters.
- **Semantics:** Landmark regions, logical heading order, correct **button vs link** usage.
- **Screen readers:** Meaningful image alternatives; ARIA only where native HTML is insufficient.

### Implementation Notes (this repo)

- Storefront follows **`@medusajs/ui`** components and **`@medusajs/ui-preset`** Tailwind tokens (see `storefront/tailwind.config.js`).
- For Medusa storefront patterns (SDK, cart, checkout), align with **building-storefronts** and **storefront-best-practices** skills.

### Design Principles

1. **Quiet UI, loud product** — One focal story per view; photography and product detail first.
2. **Frictionless paths** — Fewer decisions per screen; fast paths to size/color and checkout.
3. **Token-first color** — Medusa UI semantic tokens for surfaces, text, and borders; avoid one-off palette sprawl.
4. **Calm, never frantic** — No manufactured urgency; trust through clarity and restraint.
5. **Accessibility is shipping criteria** — Motion, contrast, keyboard, and semantics are non-negotiable.
